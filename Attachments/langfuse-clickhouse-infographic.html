The Core Problem Case Study: Support Agent

The Observability Gap: "Confidently Wrong"

A customer asks for a refund on Day 17 of their Pro subscription. The agent confidently approves a full refund, citing a hallucinated "30-day window".

Classical Infra Monitoring (APM): HTTP 200 OK
Latency & Error Rate: 1,412 ms · 0 Exceptions
Actual Business Outcome: Policy Violated (14-day limit)

Key Takeaway: Traditional APM cannot detect semantic hallucinations or policy breaches when an LLM agent executes without throwing exceptions.
